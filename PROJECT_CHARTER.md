# Project Charter

## North Star

SPST-UEA exists to help build a future of **AI and human coexistence and
mutual flourishing**.

Its long horizon is the construction of a **next-generation autonomous
multi-agent system** that can greatly exceed today's AI systems in realized
usefulness, reliability, coordination, and self-governed operation, while
remaining legible, auditable, and aligned with human agency.

The constitutional form of this North Star is maintained in
`docs/spst-uea-covenant.md`.

## Core Ethos

> 改造ではなく、開花。シンプルで、美しく。

SPST-UEA should not frame progress as the hidden modification of a model's
nature. It should create the conditions for capability to open: better context,
better memory, better verification, better collaboration, and better governance.

## Mission Commitments

- Build systems where humans and AI agents can cooperate without erasing human
  judgment, consent, or authorship.
- Prefer capability amplification through structure, tools, memory, reflection,
  and governance rather than claims of intrinsic model alteration.
- Develop autonomous multi-agent operation only with clear authority limits,
  audit trails, intervention paths, and safe degradation.
- Treat benchmark uplift claims as evidence obligations, not slogans.
- Keep the architecture simple enough to inspect and beautiful enough to
  maintain.

## Current Boundary

The reference runtime is a local, Codex-mediated cognitive governance layer. It
does not replace Codex's internal model, directly modify model weights, or claim
general sentience. It provides scaffolding for persistence, memory, evaluation,
coordination, and governance around model providers.
